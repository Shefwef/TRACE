# TRACE Consulting — Demonstration Flow

This is a concise, presentation-ready route through the completed website. It is designed for a five-to-seven-minute walkthrough with leadership, partners, or internal stakeholders.

| Moment | What to show | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Opening | Open the homepage and pause for five seconds. The six hero stories advance automatically. | “The entry experience introduces TRACE’s six operating priorities through Bangladesh-relevant imagery, then advances automatically every five seconds.” |
| 2. Control | Use an unobtrusive arrow or story indicator once. | “Visitors can still move directly to a specific story; manual selection restarts the five-second sequence.” |
| 3. Positioning | Scroll to How TRACE Works, the integrated Evidence–Delivery–Ownership entities, and What Sets TRACE Apart. | “The site explains TRACE as a connected system rather than a conventional service directory.” |
| 4. Services | Show the elevated Our Services cards and open one service detail page. | “Each service links to a briefing with overview, service inclusions, work delivered, experts, and related records.” |
| 5. Projects | Return to Featured Projects and let the accordion move, then open a project detail page. | “Project pages organise the available record as a briefing, with facts, contributors, connected work, and insights.” |
| 6. Insights | Select a Featured Insight, preview it, and open the internal detail page or original source action. | “Every curated insight retains its original TRACE article, PDF, or video source link.” |
| 7. Innovations | Hover or focus an innovation panel. | “The products use small, purposeful motion to reveal additional context without overwhelming an institutional audience.” |
| 8. People and partners | Show the moving People rail, then the partner rail and expandable map. | “The page combines relevant expertise, partner ecosystem, and office context without introducing separate visual clutter.” |
| 9. Navigation proof | Open a service, project, team, or insight page from a lower homepage position. | “Each route now opens from the top of its page, including direct detail links.” |
| 10. About and contact | Open About, then Contact. | “The About page provides the firm profile, mission, commitments, framework, and distinction areas. The visible contact form needs a production email endpoint before public launch.” |

## Before demonstrating

Use the published URL in a normal browser tab and let the homepage remain open for at least six seconds before beginning. The hero timer intentionally does not animate when the browser or operating system has **Reduce Motion** enabled; this preserves accessibility. For a motion demonstration, use a profile where reduced motion is turned off.

Use the desktop view for the main presentation, then briefly open a mobile viewport to show that the system stacks into touch-friendly layouts. Avoid claiming that the Contact form delivers messages until the proposed server-side mail integration has been completed.
